Cricket Ka Poora Itihaas: Gaon Ke Khel Se Duniya Ke Sabse Bade Junoon Tak

Published: 5 Nov 2026 | By: SFA Earn | Read Time: 12 Min

Cricket aaj sirf ek khel nahi hai. India me ye ek dharam hai, ek emotion hai, ek festival hai. Jab India-Pakistan ka match hota hai toh poora desh ruk jata hai. Galiya khaali ho jaati hai, TV ke saamne bheed lag jaati hai. Lekin kya tumhe pata hai is khel ki shuruaat 500 saal pehle England ke ek chhote se gaon me bachcho ke khel se hui thi? Aaj is article me hum cricket ki poori kahani jaanenge - uski shuruaat se leke IPL aur T20 World Cup tak.

1. Cricket Ki Shuruaat - 1500s Me Kaise Bana?

Maana jata hai ki cricket ki shuruaat 1550 ke aas-paas South-East England me hui. Us waqt bachche ek lakdi ke dante se ek ball ko maarte the. Us khel ko 'Creckett' bolte the. 1598 me pehli baar likhit roop me cricket ka naam aaya. 1611 me pehli baar bade logo ne cricket khela.

Us waqt ka bat aaj jaisa seedha nahi tha, hockey jaisa curved hota tha. Ball ko zameen pe ghuma ke feka jata tha. Wicket bhi aaj jaisa 3 stump wala nahi tha, 2 stump hote the. Dheere dheere niyam bante gaye.

1709 me pehla recorded match Kent aur London ke beech khela gaya. 1744 me pehli baar cricket ke niyam likhe gaye. 1787 me MCC yaani Marylebone Cricket Club bana. Isi club ne cricket ke niyam banaye jo aaj bhi lagbhag wahi chalte hai.

2. Lords - Cricket Ka Mecca

1814 me Lords ka maidan bana. Isko Cricket ka Mecca bolte hai. Aaj bhi har cricketer ka sapna hota hai Lords me century marna aur apna naam Honours Board pe likhwana. Sachin Tendulkar ka bhi Lords me century nahi hai, isliye unko bhi iska afsos rehta hai.

1844 me pehla International match USA aur Canada ke beech hua. Lekin asli Test cricket ki shuruaat 1877 me hui jab Australia aur England ke beech pehla Test match Melbourne me khela gaya. Australia ne wo match 45 run se jeeta. Tab se Ashes series shuru hui jo aaj bhi duniya ki sabse badi Test rivalry hai.

3. India Me Cricket Kaise Aaya?

India me cricket British lekar aaye. 1721 me pehli baar India me cricket khela gaya Gujarat ke Kutch me. 1848 me Parsi community ne pehla cricket club banaya - Oriental Cricket Club, Mumbai me. 1933 me India ne apna pehla Test match England ke khilaf Bombay ke Gymkhana Ground pe khela. CK Nayudu India ke pehle captain the.

1983 ne sab badal diya. Kapil Dev ki team ne World Cup jeeta. Us waqt kisi ko umeed nahi thi. West Indies jaisi unbeatable team ko final me 43 run se hara diya. Us din ke baad India me cricket dharam ban gaya. Gali-gali me bachche cricket khelne lage. Sachin Tendulkar jaise bachche usi World Cup ko dekh ke cricketer bane.

4. One Day Aur T20 Ka Dhamaka

1971 me pehla ODI match hua. 1975 me pehla World Cup hua. Pehle 60 over ka match hota tha, phir 50 over ka ho gaya. 2003 me T20 aaya aur 2007 me pehla T20 World Cup India ne jeeta Dhoni ki captaincy me. Us final me Misbah ka scoop aur Sreesanth ka catch aaj bhi sabko yaad hai.

2008 me IPL shuru hua aur cricket poora business ban gaya. Aaj IPL duniya ki sabse ameer league hai. Ek player 27 crore me bikta hai. IPL ne India ko bench strength di hai. Jasprit Bumrah, Hardik Pandya, Suryakumar Yadav, Rinku Singh - ye sab IPL ki hi den hai.

5. World Cup Winners Ki Poori List

ODI World Cup: 1975 West Indies, 1979 West Indies, 1983 India, 1987 Australia, 1992 Pakistan, 1996 Sri Lanka, 1999 Australia, 2003 Australia, 2007 Australia, 2011 India, 2015 Australia, 2019 England, 2023 Australia.

T20 World Cup: 2007 India, 2009 Pakistan, 2010 England, 2012 West Indies, 2014 Sri Lanka, 2016 West Indies, 2021 Australia, 2022 England, 2024 India. India ne 2024 me South Africa ko harake 17 saal baad phir se trophy jeeti. Rohit Sharma ki captaincy me wo jeet historic thi.

6. Legends - Jinhe Duniya Kabhi Nahi Bhulegi

Don Bradman ka average 99.94 aaj tak koi tod nahi paya. Sachin Tendulkar ne 100 century mari - ye record shayad kabhi na toote. Virat Kohli ne chase karna ek art bana diya. MS Dhoni ne captaincy ka matlab badal diya. Rohit Sharma ke 3 double century ODI me aaj bhi record hai.

Yuvraj Singh ne 6 ball pe 6 six maare - ye record amar hai. Chris Gayle ne T20 me tufaan machaya. Jasprit Bumrah ki yorker duniya ki sabse khatarnak yorker hai. Ab nayi generation me Abhishek Sharma, Tilak Varma, Shubman Gill aag laga rahe hai.

7. Cricket Ke Rules - Aasan Bhasha Me

Cricket me 2 team hoti hai, 11-11 player. Ek team batting karti hai, dusri bowling. Batsman ko run banana hota hai. Bowler ko wicket lena hota hai. Test me 5 din ka match, ODI me 50 over, T20 me 20 over. Jo jyada run banayega wo jeetega.

Out hone ke tarike - Bowled, Caught, LBW, Run Out, Stumped, Hit Wicket. Powerplay, Free Hit, Super Over jaise niyam ne cricket ko aur romanchak bana diya hai. Agar tum naye ho toh pehle T20 dekhna shuru karo, phir ODI, phir Test ka maza aayega.

Ab cricket me technology bhi aa gayi - DRS, Hawk-Eye, Snicko, LED stumps. Pehle umpire ka faisla aakhiri hota tha, ab 3rd umpire bhi hai. Isse galat faisle kam ho gaye hai.

8. IPL 2026 - Kaun Banega Champion?

IPL 2026 me 10 teams hai. Mumbai Indians 5 baar champion, Chennai Super Kings 5 baar champion, RCB 2025 me pehli baar champion bani. KKR, SRH, DC, PBKS, RR, LSG, GT - har team ke paas apne star hai. Is baar RCB, MI aur SRH sabse strong lag rahi hai.

IPL me sirf paisa nahi, strategy bhi chahiye. Kaun sa player kis pitch pe chalega, kaun sa bowler death over me accha hai - ye sab captain ko sochna padta hai. Auction me 100 crore se jyada kharch hota hai.

9. Mahila Cricket Aur Aane Wala Kal

Ab cricket Olympics me bhi aa raha hai. 2028 Los Angeles Olympics me cricket hoga. Mahila cricket bhi bahut tezi se badh raha hai. WPL ne ladkiyo ko bhi superstar bana diya hai. Smriti Mandhana, Harmanpreet Kaur, Shafali Verma aaj ghar-ghar me famous hai.

Aane wale 10 saal me cricket 200 desho me khela jayega. T10, The Hundred jaise naye format bhi aa rahe hai. Cricket ab sirf 11 logo ka khel nahi, 140 crore logo ki feeling hai.

10. Conclusion

Cricket me har ball pe kuch naya hota hai. Kabhi 1 ball pe 6 run chahiye, kabhi 1 ball pe 1 wicket. Ye romanch kisi aur khel me nahi hai. Isliye cricket ko Gentleman's Game bolte hai, lekin ab ye Junoon ka game bhi ban gaya hai.
